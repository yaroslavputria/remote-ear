RemoteEar — Android
One screen, ten states, and the wording that keeps them honest
Dark is the primary appearance. Every state is distinguished by shape, text and colour at once, so it survives a dark room, a glance, and colour blindness. Nothing below implies detection or alerting.

Colour-blind check
Teal and amber collapse toward each other under deuteranopia, so no state is identified by dot colour alone: listening is a ring with a filled centre, paused is two bars, error is a filled circle with a mark, idle is a hollow ring. State text sits at 34 px next to it.
How dim Listening can go
After 20 s without touch the screen drops to a black surface with the state text at #3E8C7D and Stop at #5C6564 — roughly 5:1 and 4.6:1 on black, still legible at the lowest system brightness while emitting about a tenth of the light of the normal state. Nothing below the Stop button is drawn. Any touch restores the full screen.

A sound field rather than an ear, a microphone or an eye: concentric rings around a filled centre, the room and the point you are listening from. It reads at 24 px, holds up in monochrome, and carries no surveillance language. The outer ring drops out at the smallest size; the two inner shapes are the icon.
